# Community code of conduct

daakDİKTE welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, assume good intent while
still addressing harmful impact, and make room for people who communicate in a
different language or style. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, doxxing and publishing another person's recordings or transcripts
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct problems
privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than starting a public
argument. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
